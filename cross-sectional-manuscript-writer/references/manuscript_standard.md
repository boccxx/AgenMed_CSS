# Manuscript standard

Write from frozen upstream evidence. The manuscript is a projection of
contracts and registries, not a place to repair missing design or analysis.

## Input gate

Resolve and hash the available review, protocol, estimand, SAP, analysis,
release-quality, result, table, figure, citation, and binding-map objects.
Stop or downgrade when an input is missing, stale, unfrozen, or inconsistent.

The manuscript contract must declare:

- project and manuscript mode;
- claim ceiling;
- allowed result, table, figure, and citation IDs;
- source paths and hashes;
- release status and any downgrade.

## Writing sequence

1. Confirm mode, target journal, design, and deliverables.
2. Bind claims to allowed registry objects.
3. Draft the scaffold and section order.
4. Write Introduction and Discussion claims from verified citations.
5. Write Methods from executed protocol, SAP, and analysis evidence.
6. Write Results only from registered results, tables, and figures.
7. Resolve display paths through registries.
8. Run contract, mode, binding, quality, and placeholder checks.
9. Inspect DOCX/PDF pagination, tables, figures, captions, and references.

## Claim boundaries

- Identify the study as cross-sectional and preserve its temporal limits.
- Never turn association into causation or OR into PR/RR.
- Never invent authorship, ethics approval, registration, funding, conflicts,
  sample counts, estimates, citations, tables, or figures.
- Describe only executed methods and label departures from the SAP.
- Every numeric sentence must bind to a result, table, or figure row.
- Synthetic, pilot, and workflow-method outputs must be labelled as such and
  must not be written as population evidence.

## Release gate

Release only when the body, section, citation, display, sequence, and
no-placeholder checks pass for the requested format. A shortened journal
format is acceptable only when its exact scope is declared. Local checks do
not replace current journal, STROBE, or SAMPL requirements.
