Please Select the Framework Appropriate for Your Research Question

PECO Framework

Applicable Scenarios: Investigating associations between exposure and outcome or conducting inter-group comparisons

Four Components:

  • • P (Population) - Population: Whom do you wish to study?
  • • E (Exposure) - Exposure: What is the core factor under investigation?
  • • C (Comparison) - Comparison: What is the reference group for the exposed group?
  • • O (Outcome) - Outcome: What is the health condition or event of interest?

CoCoPop Framework

Applicable Scenarios: Estimating the prevalence of a condition in a specific population

Three Components:

  • • Co (Condition) - Condition: What is the specific disease, symptom, or state under investigation?
  • • Co (Context) - Context: In what environment or setting does the condition occur?
  • • Pop (Population) - Population: What is the specific target population for your study?

SPIDER Framework

Applicable Scenarios: Conducting qualitative exploration of people's experiences and perspectives to inform subsequent quantitative research

Five Components:

  • • S (Sample) - Sample: Who constitutes the study sample?
  • • PI (Phenomenon of Interest) - Phenomenon of Interest: What is the core phenomenon of interest?
  • • D (Design) - Design: What design will the study employ?
  • • E (Evaluation) - Evaluation: What outcomes do you wish to evaluate or understand?
  • • R (Research type) - Research Type: What type of research does this represent?

PECO and CoCoPop Framework (Hybrid)

Applicable Scenarios: Studies involving both investigation of exposure-outcome associations/inter-group comparisons and estimation of prevalence of a condition in a specific population

Included Components: All elements of PECO + All elements of CoCoPop
